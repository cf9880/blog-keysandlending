- **VA loans:** no down payment for eligible buyers and no monthly mortgage insurance. The funding fee is 2.15% for first use with less than 5% down, and waived for many disabled veterans.

*Figures from published 2026 BAH tables. Confirm your exact rate with the [Defense Travel Management Office BAH calculator](https://www.travel.dod.mil/Allowances/Basic-Allowance-for-Housing/BAH-Rate-Lookup/).*

Lenders can count BAH as income when you qualify. BAH isn't taxed, so many lenders "gross it up," which can help your debt-to-income ratio.

## How Do VA Loan Costs Work?

The VA funding fee depends on your down payment and whether you've used your benefit before ([VA.gov](https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/funding-fee-and-closing-costs/)):

| Down payment | First use | Subsequent use |
|---|---|---|
| Less than 5% | 2.15% | 3.3% |
| 5% to less than 10% | 1.5% | 1.5% |
| 10% or more | 1.25% | 1.25% |

You don't pay the funding fee if you receive VA disability compensation or are a Purple Heart recipient, among other exemptions. Sellers can pay up to 4% in concessions, which can cover items like the funding fee or prepaid costs. Mortgage News Daily reported a 30-year VA average of **6.98%** on October 26, 2026.
