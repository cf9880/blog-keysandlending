## Key Takeaways

- **Down payment:** 3.5% with a 580+ credit score; 10% for scores of 500 to 579 (HUD).
- **2026 FHA limits:** $541,287 in Escambia and Santa Rosa; $603,750 in Okaloosa (HUD).
- **Mortgage insurance:** 1.75% upfront plus 0.55% annual for most borrowers (HUD).
- **2027 limits are coming.** They follow FHFA's conforming limit, which was not yet announced as of November 19, 2026.
- **Primary residence only.** FHA isn't for vacation rentals or investment homes.

## What Is an FHA Loan?

An **FHA loan** is a mortgage insured by the Federal Housing Administration, part of HUD. Because FHA insures the lender against loss, these loans often allow lower down payments and more flexible credit guidelines than many conventional loans.

**Mortgage insurance premium (MIP)** is the cost of that insurance. FHA has an upfront MIP paid at closing (or financed into the loan) and an annual MIP split into your monthly payment.

## What Are the 2026 FHA Loan Limits on the Emerald Coast?

HUD sets FHA limits by county. For 2026, the national floor for a one-unit home is $541,287, which is 65% of the $832,750 conforming limit, according to [HUD Mortgagee Letter 2025-23](https://www.hud.gov/sites/default/files/hudclips/documents/2025-23hsgml.pdf). Okaloosa County is above the floor, per HUD's [2026 list of areas above the floor](https://www.hud.gov/sites/dfiles/SFH/documents/FHA-2026-Areas-Above-Floor-and-Below-Ceiling.pdf).

| --- | --- | --- |
| Escambia | $541,287 | $693,050 |
| Santa Rosa | $541,287 | $693,050 |
| Okaloosa | $603,750 | $772,900 |

These limits cover the large majority of homes in the area. Our [Emerald Coast housing market update](/posts/emerald-coast-housing-market-november-2026/) reported September 2026 single-family medians of $307,900 in Escambia, $353,455 in Santa Rosa and $385,000 in Okaloosa, based on Momentum Realty's market reports.

## How Much Does an FHA Loan Cost?

- **Upfront MIP: 1.75%** of the base loan amount, usually financed.
- **Annual MIP: 0.55%** for most borrowers, after HUD's 2023 reduction ([Mortgagee Letter 2023-05](https://www.hud.gov/sites/default/files/OCHCO/documents/2023-05hsgml.pdf)). With less than 10% down, it generally lasts for the life of the loan.
- **Interest rate:** set by the lender. Mortgage News Daily's index put the 30-year FHA rate at 7.04% on November 18, 2026, versus 7.46% for a top-tier 30-year conventional loan. Freddie Mac's 30-year conventional average was 7.11% on November 12, 2026. Your rate depends on your credit and loan details.

Jay's tip: compare FHA side by side with conventional. With strong credit and a larger down payment, conventional mortgage insurance may cost less over time. With a lower score, FHA often comes out ahead.

## What Does an FHA Appraisal Look For?

FHA appraisals check value and also whether the home meets HUD's minimum property standards for safety, security and soundness. Angee's tip from the buyer side: older homes with peeling paint, roof issues or missing handrails can lead to required repairs before closing. On the Emerald Coast, roof age and wind protection also matter for insurance. We'll flag potential issues early so they don't surprise you during the appraisal.

## Can Sellers Help With Closing Costs on an FHA Loan?

Yes. FHA allows sellers and other interested parties to contribute toward your closing costs, up to 6% of the sales price. With many local homes taking five weeks or more to sell this fall, sellers are often open to that conversation. Gift funds from family can also be used for the down payment, which can be a timely topic over Thanksgiving.

## Will FHA Loan Limits Change in 2027?

Most likely, but not yet. HUD sets FHA limits based on the conforming loan limit, and FHFA had not announced the 2027 conforming limit as of November 19, 2026. HUD usually follows with new FHA limits, which typically apply to case numbers assigned on or after January 1.
