## How Does DSCR Math Work on the Emerald Coast?

**DSCR = monthly rent ÷ monthly PITIA** (principal, interest, taxes, insurance, and association dues). A ratio of 1.0 means the rent covers the payment. Many lenders look for 1.0 or higher.

On the Gulf, the insurance line can be the difference between a ratio that works and one that does not. Order wind and homeowners quotes early. If the property is a condo or in an HOA, association dues belong in the same calculation.

## What About Military Rentals and Vacation Use?

Long-term rentals near bases such as Eglin AFB and Hurlburt Field are a familiar Emerald Coast story, but each property still has to stand on its lease or rent schedule. A vacation-style property is a different file. Some DSCR lenders accept short-term rental income; others want a longer-term lease. Local governments and HOAs may also limit or license short-term rentals.

Confirm legal use first. Then decide whether the loan should be underwritten on a lease or on short-term income. ### Are short-term rentals easy to finance here?

Not automatically. Some DSCR lenders accept short-term rental income and others do not. Cities, counties, and HOAs also set their own rules. Confirm legal use before you underwrite an offer on vacation-season rent.

### How does insurance affect an Emerald Coast DSCR loan?

Wind and homeowners insurance are part of PITIA, the monthly payment used to calculate DSCR. Higher Gulf Coast premiums can lower the ratio, so get quotes early.

### Can I buy the property in an LLC?

Many DSCR programs allow an LLC to hold the loan, usually with a personal guarantee. Requirements vary by lender, so plan the entity before you go under contract.
