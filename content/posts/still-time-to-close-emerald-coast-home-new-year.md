A 2026 Intercontinental Exchange study cited by Zillow found that the average purchase closing takes about 37 days, and conventional loans often close 30 to 45 days after the contract is signed. ## Key Takeaways

- **Plan on 30 to 45 days** from an accepted contract, with a national average near 37 days.
- **Thanksgiving (November 26) is likely too soon** to start a new purchase. December 31 is the more realistic target.
- **Order insurance early.** Wind coverage is a frequent Gulf Coast delay.
- **Military report dates should drive the contract date**, not the other way around.

## How Many Days Are Left?

From mid-November:

- **Thanksgiving, November 26:** about 11 days, shorter than a typical mortgage close.
- **Christmas, December 25:** about 40 days, possible on a 30- to 45-day contract but tight.
- **December 31:** about 46 days, a more comfortable window if documents and insurance stay on schedule.

[Zillow](https://www.zillow.com/learn/how-long-does-it-take-to-close-on-a-house/), citing a 2026 ICE study, reports an average close of about 37 days. That is a national figure, not an Emerald Coast promise. A late insurance binder or a delayed appraisal can push the file into January.

If you already have a contract, we can look at the date against Thanksgiving and Christmas closures. If you are just starting, we will plan for year-end, not a pre-Thanksgiving move-in.
