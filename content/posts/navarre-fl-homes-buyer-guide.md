**Navarre, FL homes sold for a median of about $421,000 in September 2026,** and with roughly **5.8 months of supply**, buyers have more room to negotiate than they've had in a while. Whether you want a full-time family home on the mainland, a beach getaway, or a vacation rental, Navarre offers options at prices often below nearby beach towns.

We're **Angee Kent, REALTOR®, LPT Realty** and **Jay Spurlin (Coastal Funding Corporation, NMLS 183681)**. Here's our team's take on Navarre prices, loan choices, and the small-town beach life that keeps people coming back.

## Key takeaways

- **Median sale price:** $421,000 in ZIP 32566 (September 2026, Pensacola MLS).
- **More choices:** 185 homes for sale vs. 32 sales, about 5.8 months of supply.
- **Gap to watch:** median list price $550,000 vs. median sale price $421,000.
- **Loan fit:** primary-home, second-home, and DSCR investment loans each have different rules.
- **Local gems:** a 1,545-foot fishing pier and the Navarre Beach Sea Turtle Conservation Center.

## Which loan fits your Navarre plans?

Start with one question: **how will you use the home?**

| Use | Loan types | Typical notes |
|---|---|---|
| Live there full time | Conventional, FHA, VA, USDA (eligible areas) | Lowest down payments |
| Part-time getaway | Second-home conventional | Often 10% down; you must use it yourself |
| Rental or vacation rental | Conventional investment, DSCR | Larger down payment; rent can help you qualify |

- **Primary home:** conventional from 3% down for qualified buyers, FHA at 3.5% down (2026 limit $541,287 in Santa Rosa County), and VA at $0 down for eligible military families. Navarre is a popular choice for families connected to Hurlburt Field and Eglin AFB.
- **Second home:** for a home you'll personally use part of the year. It can't be run as a full-time rental.
- **DSCR loan:** Coastal Funding offers DSCR loans nationwide for investment properties.

**DSCR (definition):** debt service coverage ratio, which is the property's monthly rent divided by its monthly mortgage payment, taxes, insurance, and HOA dues. A ratio of 1.0 or higher means the rent covers the costs.

**Lender-side tip:** for a vacation rental, have a realistic rent estimate and an insurance quote in hand before you write an offer. Those two numbers drive DSCR approval.

## What should Navarre buyers know about the beach and the storm?

**Navarre Beach is leasehold land.** According to the U.S. Department of the Interior, Navarre Beach is owned by Escambia County and was leased to Santa Rosa County in 1956. That's why many beach properties are sold as leasehold interests, similar to Pensacola Beach. Our [Pensacola Beach leasehold guide](../pensacola-beach-leasehold-explained/) explains what that means for financing. Mainland Navarre homes are generally fee simple, but your title company will confirm.

**After Hurricane Isaias:** Santa Rosa County lifted its evacuation orders on October 12, and the Navarre Beach boil-water notice was lifted on October 14, 2026 (WUWF). The Navarre Beach Area Chamber of Commerce scheduled a hurricane recovery resource fair for **October 23**. Before buying, get a thorough inspection, ask for repair records, and price wind and flood insurance early.

## Fun things to love about Navarre

- **Navarre Beach Fishing Pier:** at **1,545 feet**, it's billed as the longest fishing pier in Florida and on the Gulf. Check its status before visiting while post-storm repairs continue.
- **Navarre Beach Sea Turtle Conservation Center:** at 8740 Gulf Blvd. in Navarre Beach Marine Park. From November through May, it's open Tuesday to Saturday, noon to 4 p.m. Admission is $5, or $4 for military and seniors 65+.
- **Quiet beach vibe:** fewer high-rises and a slower pace than many Gulf Coast towns.
