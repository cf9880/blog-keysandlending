**A realtor and lender team in Pensacola can shorten your path to closing by keeping offers, financing, and deadlines in sync.** Many financed purchases close in about **30 to 45 days** after the contract is signed. When your agent and loan officer share information from day one, fewer things slip, and sellers take your offer more seriously.

We're **Angee Kent with LPT Realty** and **Jay Spurlin with Coastal Funding Corporation (NMLS 183681)**. Teaming up is the whole idea behind Keys and Lending. Here's what that looks like, step by step, for buyers in Pensacola, Gulf Breeze, and Navarre.

## Key takeaways

- **Preapproval first:** it sets your budget and makes your offer stronger.
- **Typical timeline:** about 30–45 days from contract to closing for many financed purchases.
- **Shared calendar:** inspection, appraisal, financing, and insurance deadlines tracked together.
- **Fall advantage:** Pensacola-area pending sales were down 8.9% year over year in September 2026 (PAR).
- **Your choice:** you can always pick any agent or lender. A team is a convenience, not a requirement.

## What does a realtor and lender team actually do?

**Real estate agent (Angee's side):**

- Finds homes that match your budget and must-haves
- Pulls recent sales to help you price an offer
- Negotiates price, repairs, and seller credits
- Tracks inspection and contract deadlines

**Loan officer (Jay's side):**

- Reviews credit, income, and assets for a real **preapproval**
- Compares loan programs and lenders (Coastal Funding is a broker)
- Explains payment, cash to close, and rate lock options
- Manages the appraisal, underwriting, and closing documents

**Together:** we check the payment on a specific home before you offer, confirm the seller credit you're asking for actually fits your loan, and talk through surprises the same day.

**Preapproval (definition):** a lender's written conditional approval after reviewing your credit, income, and assets. It's stronger than a **prequalification**, which is a quick estimate based on what you share.

## What is the Pensacola home buying timeline?

Here's a typical path for a financed purchase. Every deal is different.

| Step | Typical timing | Who leads |
|---|---|---|
| Preapproval | 1–3 days | Lender |
| Home search and offer | Varies | Agent |
| Inspection period | Often 7–15 days | Agent + buyer |
| Appraisal ordered | First week under contract | Lender |
| Insurance bound | Before closing | Buyer, with agent and lender |
| Underwriting and clear to close | About 3–5 weeks | Lender |
| Closing | About 30–45 days after contract | Title company |

**Contingency (definition):** a contract condition, such as inspection, appraisal, or financing, that must be met for the sale to move forward.

## Why does teamwork matter more right now?

**Rates are higher.** Freddie Mac's 30-year average was **7.40%** on October 15, 2026, up from 6.27% a year earlier. Small changes in price, credits, or rate buydowns can change your payment a lot, so we run the numbers before you offer.

**Sellers are negotiating.** The Pensacola Association of REALTORS® reported **235 pending sales** in September 2026, down 8.9% from a year earlier, and single-family inventory of 3,114 homes. Momentum Realty's market report found that 49% of active Pensacola MLS listings had cut their price as of October 20.

**Storm recovery adds steps.** After Hurricane Isaias hit on October 9, many deals need re-inspections, updated appraisals, and extra insurance documents. When your agent and lender coordinate, those steps happen in parallel instead of one after another.

## What makes an offer stronger without overpaying?

1. **A full preapproval letter** matched to the offer price.
2. **A clean, realistic timeline**, such as a 30- to 45-day close.
3. **Smart use of seller credits.** In a slower market, asking for closing cost help or a rate buydown can beat a price cut.
4. **Insurance quotes early**, so there are no last-minute surprises.
5. **Quick responses.** Answering lender and agent requests the same day keeps everything on track.

## Frequently asked questions

**How long does it take to close on a house in Pensacola?**
Many financed purchases close in about 30 to 45 days after the contract. Storm-related steps can add time.

**What is the difference between prequalification and preapproval?**
A prequalification is a quick estimate. A preapproval means the lender reviewed your credit, income, and assets.

**Why should my realtor and lender work together?**
Shared deadlines and information mean stronger offers and fewer surprises.

**Can I choose my own lender and agent?**
Always. You're free to choose and compare. A team is a convenience, never a requirement.

**Is fall a good time to buy in Pensacola?**
Often, yes. Less competition and more motivated sellers give buyers room to negotiate.
