**The Emerald Coast housing market is balanced to buyer-leaning heading into winter 2026. September single-family medians were $307,900 in Escambia County, $353,455 in Santa Rosa County, and $385,000 in Okaloosa County. Homes took about five to seven weeks to sell, and 30-year rates eased to about 7.24%.**

We're **Angee Kent, REALTOR® with LPT Realty**, and **Jay Spurlin, Mortgage Loan Originator (NMLS 183681)** with Coastal Funding Corporation. Each month we look at the numbers together, so you get the house side and the loan side in one place. Here's what the latest data says about Escambia, Santa Rosa, and Okaloosa Counties.

## Key Takeaways

- **Prices are mixed:** Okaloosa's median rose 10.0% from a year earlier, Escambia's rose 2.6%, and Santa Rosa's dipped 0.4%.
- **Homes sell in about 36 to 43 days** at the median, so buyers rarely need to rush.
- **Supply favors buyers more in Okaloosa:** about 6.2 months in the Emerald Coast MLS area, versus about 3.2 months in the Pensacola MLS area.
- **Rates eased in October:** Freddie Mac's 30-year average was 7.24% on Oct. 29, down from 7.32% the week before.
- **Late fall is a good time to negotiate,** especially for seller help with closing costs.

*Sources: Momentum Realty [Pensacola Market Pulse](https://movewithmomentum.com/reports/pulse/pensacola/2026-09) (Escambia and Santa Rosa) and [Emerald Coast Market Pulse](https://movewithmomentum.com/reports/pulse/emerald-coast/2026-09) (Okaloosa), September 2026.*

Okaloosa had 233 single-family closings in September. Its strong yearly gain partly reflects which homes sold that month, so one month's median can swing. Look at the trend over several months, not just one report.

## Is It a Buyer's or Seller's Market?

It depends on where you look:

- **Escambia and Santa Rosa** have about 3.2 months of supply across the Pensacola MLS area. That's a fairly balanced market. Well-priced homes still sell, but buyers can negotiate.
- **Okaloosa** sits in the Emerald Coast MLS area, which had about 6.2 months of supply for single-family homes. That leans toward buyers, with more choice and more room on price and terms.

Angee's view from the field: sellers who price right from day one are still getting offers. Homes that start too high tend to sit, and those are where buyers find the best deals.

The Federal Reserve held rates steady on Oct. 28, 2026, but two officials voted for a hike, so rates could stay volatile into December. Jay's take: lock when the payment fits your budget, and ask about float-down options if your lender offers one.

## What Does This Mean for Buyers?

- **Negotiate closing costs.** With homes taking five or more weeks to sell, sellers are often open to paying part of your costs or a rate buydown.
- **Shop by commute.** Military families near Eglin AFB and Hurlburt Field should read our [Eglin and Hurlburt PCS guide](/posts/buying-home-near-eglin-afb-hurlburt-field/).
- **Compare counties.** Santa Rosa's prices are flat from last year, which can open doors in places like Navarre. See our [Navarre homes buyer guide](/posts/navarre-fl-homes-buyer-guide/).
- **Get preapproved before the holidays.** Fewer buyers shop between Thanksgiving and New Year's, and a ready buyer stands out.

## What Does This Mean for Sellers?

- **Price to the current market,** not to the spring 2022 peak.
- **Prepare the home for winter showings** with good lighting, a clean yard, and repairs done up front.
- **Expect negotiation.** Offering to help with a buyer's closing costs can attract more offers than a price cut.
