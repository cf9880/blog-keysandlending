## What makes the best mortgage lender in Gulf Breeze and Navarre?

No lender is best for everyone. A great fit for a $1 million soundfront jumbo loan might not be the right fit for a first-time VA buyer near Hurlburt Field. Judge every lender on:

1. **Loan options:** conventional, FHA, VA, USDA, jumbo, and investment loans.
2. **Total cost:** rate, APR, points, and lender fees on the Loan Estimate.
3. **Coastal know-how:** flood zones, wind insurance, condo approvals, and Pensacola Beach and Navarre Beach leaseholds.
4. **Teamwork:** how well the lender communicates with your agent and title company.
5. **Service:** a real person who answers your questions, including outside banker's hours.

## How to choose a mortgage lender: a buyer's checklist

1. **Check your credit** and set a comfortable payment, including insurance and HOA dues.
2. **Get three quotes the same day** with the same loan amount, down payment, and lock period.
3. **Compare Loan Estimates** line by line ([CFPB](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/owning-a-home/loan-estimate/)).
4. **Verify licenses** on [NMLS Consumer Access](https://www.nmlsconsumeraccess.org/).
5. **Quote insurance early,** including flood, before you write an offer.
6. **Ask your REALTOR®** which lenders close smoothly.
7. **Get fully preapproved** so sellers take your offer seriously.

### Questions to ask your lender

- Which loan fits me best, and what will my payment be?
- What are the rate, APR, and total lender fees?
- How long is my rate lock, and what happens if a storm delays closing?
- Do you finance condos, beach leaseholds, and homes in flood zones?
- Do you offer down payment assistance options?
- How will you keep both me and my agent updated?

## Home loan options for Gulf Breeze, Navarre, and Pensacola

- **Conventional:** as little as 3% down for qualified first-time buyers; $832,750 conforming limit in 2026 ([FHFA](https://www.fhfa.gov/news/news-release/fhfa-announces-conforming-loan-limit-values-for-2026)).
- **FHA:** 3.5% down with flexible credit guidelines.
- **VA:** $0 down for eligible veterans and service members, popular with Hurlburt Field, Eglin, and NAS Pensacola families.
- **USDA:** $0 down in eligible parts of Santa Rosa County; check the [USDA eligibility map](https://eligibility.sc.egov.usda.gov/eligibility/welcomeAction.do).
- **Jumbo:** for waterfront and Pensacola Beach homes above the conforming limit.
- **Second-home and DSCR loans:** for vacation homes and rentals.
- **Down payment assistance:** offered through several of Coastal Funding's lender partners; eligibility varies.

## Refinancing: what current homeowners should know

Freddie Mac's 30-year average was **7.32%** on Oct. 22, 2026, up from 6.19% a year earlier ([Freddie Mac PMMS](https://www.freddiemac.com/pmms)). A rate-only refinance won't help many local homeowners right now, but a refinance or HELOC (home equity line of credit) can still make sense to:

- **Drop FHA mortgage insurance** by moving to conventional once you have enough equity.
- **Take cash out** for storm repairs, a new roof, or debt payoff.
- **Move from an adjustable rate** to a fixed rate.

**Break-even point (definition):** closing costs divided by monthly savings. Ask Jay for a free rate check to see if it's worth it.
