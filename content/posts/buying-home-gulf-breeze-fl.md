**What it means for you:**

- In **32563**, homes near the median still sell close to list price. Come in prepared.
- In **32561**, five months of supply and a 96% sale-to-list ratio give buyers more room to negotiate.

**Months of supply (definition):** how long it would take to sell every listed home at the current pace. Around 4–6 months is often called balanced. Lower favors sellers.

## Which mortgage fits a Gulf Breeze home?

- **Conventional:** the 2026 conforming limit is **$832,750** for a one-unit home (FHFA). Many 32563 homes and some 32561 homes fit here with as little as 3%–5% down for qualified buyers.
- **Jumbo:** for loan amounts above $832,750. Expect stronger credit, more reserves, and often 10%–20% down. Mortgage News Daily's 30-year jumbo index was **7.65%** on October 20, 2026.
- **VA:** $0 down for eligible veterans with full entitlement and no VA loan limit, which is popular with families tied to NAS Pensacola and Hurlburt Field.
- **FHA:** 3.5% down. The 2026 FHA limit for the Pensacola metro (Escambia and Santa Rosa) is $541,287 for a one-unit home.

**Jumbo loan (definition):** a mortgage larger than the conforming limit, so Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac won't buy it. Lenders set their own rules.

**Lender-side tip:** if you're close to $832,750, a slightly bigger down payment can keep you in conforming territory, which often means simpler guidelines.

## What should buyers check after Hurricane Isaias?

Isaias made landfall near Destin on October 9, 2026. WUWF reported uprooted trees in Gulf Breeze, and the city temporarily closed Shoreline Park South and other facilities for cleanup.

Before you buy:

1. **Inspect the roof, trees, and docks.** Waterfront homes can have hidden seawall or dock damage.
2. **Price insurance before you offer.** Insurers may ask for a no-damage statement or a recent inspection.
3. **Know the flood zone.** Many waterfront Gulf Breeze homes sit in FEMA flood zones, which means lenders require flood insurance.
4. **Ask for repair records** and permits from the seller.

**Realtor-side tip:** in 32561, a longer time on market can mean a seller is ready to offer credits for storm repairs or insurance costs. It never hurts to ask.

## Fun facts about Gulf Breeze

- **Naval Live Oaks Reservation** was established in **1828** as the first federally funded tree farm in the U.S. It grew live oaks for Navy shipbuilding and is now part of **Gulf Islands National Seashore**, with trails right along Highway 98. (National Park Service)
- **Gulf Breeze incorporated in 1961**, according to the City of Gulf Breeze.
- **Peninsula living:** Pensacola Bay on one side and Santa Rosa Sound on the other, with the beach a short drive across the bridge.
