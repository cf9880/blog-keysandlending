**Winter home buying in Gulf Breeze and Navarre can give you more choices and fewer bidding wars.** Santa Rosa County closed 387 home sales in December 2025, up about 17.6% from a year earlier, while the median price stayed near $343,700. With fewer tourists and buyers around, off-season sellers are often ready to talk.

We're **Angee Kent, REALTOR®, LPT Realty**, and **Jay Spurlin, Mortgage Loan Originator (NMLS 183681)** with Coastal Funding Corporation. Here's how we split the work on a holiday purchase, from first showing to keys.

## Key Takeaways

- **December is active:** 387 Santa Rosa County sales in December 2025, up about 17.6% year over year.
- **Prices held steady:** a $343,700 county median, essentially flat from December 2024.
- **Gulf Breeze moved too:** about 47 sales and a $410,000 median in December 2025, based on MLS data.
- **Rates today:** 7.33% average 30-year fixed and 7.60% jumbo (Mortgage News Daily, October 26, 2026).
- **Team timing:** go under contract by late November for a December closing.

## Is Winter a Good Time to Buy in Gulf Breeze and Navarre?

The off-season changes the mood. Summer brings vacation crowds and quick decisions. Winter brings quieter roads, easier showing schedules, and sellers who have often been on the market a while.

National data backs this up. Redfin found U.S. sellers outnumbered buyers by 45% in December 2025, the biggest gap in its records going back to 2013. Freddie Mac's [winter homebuying tips](https://myhome.freddiemac.com/blog/homebuying/buy-or-sell-in-winter) make a similar point: off-season buyers may face less competition.

Winter is also a smart time to judge a coastal home. You can see how it handles a cold front, a heavy rain, or a windy day on the sound.

## How Do Today's Mortgage Rates Change the Plan?

According to [Mortgage News Daily](https://www.mortgagenewsdaily.com/mortgage-rates/mnd) on October 26, 2026:

- 30-year fixed: **7.33%**
- 15-year fixed: **6.99%**
- FHA: **6.96%**
- VA: **6.98%**
- Jumbo: **7.60%**

Freddie Mac's [weekly survey](https://www.freddiemac.com/pmms) showed a 7.32% average 30-year rate for October 22, down from 7.40% the prior week.

**Jay's side of the plan:** get a full preapproval, not just a quick estimate. Compare conforming and jumbo pricing if you're shopping waterfront homes. Run the payment with real insurance quotes, because wind and flood premiums can change the budget as much as the rate does.

## What Can You Negotiate in the Off-Season?

**Angee's side of the plan:** when a listing has been sitting through the slow months, we look beyond price. Common winter asks include:

- **Seller credits** for closing costs or a rate buydown
- **Repairs** from inspection, especially roofs, HVAC, and anything salt air has worn down
- **Wind mitigation items** that may help lower insurance costs
- **Furniture or rental bookings** on second homes, when it makes sense
- **A closing date** that fits your move or the school calendar

For beach property, confirm the land type early. Many homes on Pensacola Beach and Navarre Beach are leasehold, not fee simple. Our [Pensacola Beach leasehold guide](/posts/pensacola-beach-leasehold-explained/) explains what that means for financing.

## How Do We Close Before the End of the Year?

1. **Week 1:** preapproval, budget, and a short list of homes.
2. **By late November:** offer accepted. Order inspection, appraisal, survey, and insurance quotes at once.
3. **Early December:** loan conditions cleared and rate locked.
4. **Mid-December:** final numbers reviewed and closing scheduled around holiday office hours.
5. **Closing week:** confirm wire instructions by phone with the title company, then do your final walkthrough.

## What Is There to Do Around Gulf Breeze and Navarre This Holiday Season?

- **Navarre Christmas Parade and Christmas in the Park:** Saturday, December 5, 2026, at 1 p.m. The parade starts at the Navarre library, and Santa photos follow at Navarre Park, 8513 Navarre Pkwy. The [Navarre Beach Area Chamber](https://business.navarrechamber.com/events/details/annual-navarre-community-christmas-parade-12-05-2026-35193) notes it's been a local tradition since 1994.
- **Gulf Breeze Reverse Holiday Parade:** Saturday, December 12, 5 to 8 p.m. You drive past the decorated displays at the Community Center, 800 Shoreline Drive. Details are on the [City of Gulf Breeze site](https://cityofgulfbreeze.us/reverse-holiday-parade/).

**History note:** Fort Pickens, at the west end of Santa Rosa Island, was finished in October 1834. In 1886, Geronimo and 15 other Apache men were imprisoned there, and their families joined them in 1887, according to the [National Park Service](https://www.nps.gov/places/000/apache-prisoners.htm). The fort is now part of Gulf Islands National Seashore.
