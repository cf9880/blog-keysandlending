## Key Takeaways

- **Crestview is the "Hub City"** of Northwest Florida, where I-10, U.S. 90, and State Road 85 meet.
- **Prices run below the coast.** Redfin's three-month median was about $277,000 through September, down 3.8% from a year earlier.
- **Buyers have time.** Homes took a median of 84 days to sell, up from 71 a year earlier, according to Redfin.
- **2026 Okaloosa County limits:** $603,750 for FHA and $832,750 for conforming loans on a one-unit home.
- **Military buyers** can compare VA loans, which often require no down payment.

## What Is Crestview Known For?

According to the [City of Crestview](https://www.cityofcrestview.org/257/History), the city was incorporated in 1916. After Okaloosa County was formed in 1915, Crestview became the county seat in 1917, and it still is today. Its name comes from its spot on a ridge between the Yellow and Shoal rivers.

The city says its elevation of 235 feet makes it one of the highest points in Florida. Crestview sits about 50 miles east of Pensacola and 30 miles inland from the Gulf. The railroad that runs through town parallels the Old Spanish Trail, the historic route between Jacksonville and El Paso.

**A Veterans Day stop:** The Okaloosa County Veterans Memorial stands next to the county courthouse downtown. It was dedicated in March 2003 to honor veterans who died in wartime. It's a meaningful place to visit this month, and a reminder of how many military families call this area home.

## Is Crestview a Good Place to Live Near Eglin AFB?

Many buyers choose Crestview for value and commute options. SR 85 runs south toward Eglin Air Force Base and the Niceville and Fort Walton Beach areas. I-10 makes trips to Pensacola, DeFuniak Springs, and beyond straightforward. Hurlburt Field is farther southwest, so test-drive your commute at rush hour before you buy.

## Which Loans Work Best in Crestview?

**VA loans.** Eligible veterans and service members often buy with no down payment and no monthly mortgage insurance. A one-time [VA funding fee](https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/funding-fee-and-closing-costs/) applies unless you're exempt. For a $300,000 home with no down payment, a 2.15% first-use funding fee would make the loan $306,450. At Mortgage News Daily's VA index of 6.92% on November 2, 2026, principal and interest would be about $2,022 a month. That's an illustration, not a quote.

**FHA loans.** Okaloosa County's 2026 FHA limit is $603,750 for one unit, per [HUD](https://entp.hud.gov/idapp/html/hicostlook.cfm). FHA allows 3.5% down with a 580 or higher credit score.

**Conventional loans.** Okaloosa County's 2026 conforming limit is $832,750. Eligible first-time buyers can put as little as 3% down, and PMI can be removed later.

**USDA loans.** Some areas outside city limits may qualify for zero-down USDA financing. Check any address on the [USDA eligibility map](https://eligibility.sc.egov.usda.gov/eligibility/welcomeAction.do).

Down payment assistance may be available through several of Jay's lender partners, depending on the program and your eligibility.

## What Should Crestview Buyers Check Before Making an Offer?

- **Insurance quotes first.** Crestview is inland, but wind coverage still matters in Florida. Ask about the roof's age and wind-mitigation features.
- **Flood zone.** Higher ground helps, but low areas near creeks can still be mapped in flood zones. Check the [FEMA Flood Map Service Center](https://msc.fema.gov/portal/home).
- **New construction details.** If you're buying from a builder, compare builder incentives with outside loan options before you sign.
- **Commute and schools.** Drive the route and confirm school zones for the specific address.
