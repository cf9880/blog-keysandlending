## Where Is Niceville, Florida?

Niceville sits in southern Okaloosa County on the north side of Choctawhatchee Bay, along Boggy Bayou and Rocky Bayou. It borders the city of Valparaiso, which sits right next to Eglin AFB. The Mid-Bay Bridge connects the area to Destin and the beaches across the bay.

That location gives Niceville a small-town feel with quick access to work at Eglin, shopping, and the Gulf. It's also home to Northwest Florida State College's main campus.

## What Is There to Do in Niceville?

- **Fred Gannon Rocky Bayou State Park:** A former World War II gunnery and bombing practice range, now shaded by longleaf pines, some over 300 years old, according to [Florida State Parks](https://www.floridastateparks.org/parks-and-trails/fred-gannon-rocky-bayou-state-park). Rocky Bayou is popular for boating, fishing, kayaking, and sunsets. The park hosts an annual Pioneer Day in November.
- **The bayous and the bay:** Boating and fishing are part of everyday life here.
- **Quick trips to Destin:** Cross the Mid-Bay Bridge for beaches, dining, and holiday shopping.
- **Arts and culture:** Northwest Florida State College's Niceville campus hosts performances and events during the year.

## What Loans Do Niceville Buyers Use?

- **VA loans:** $0 down for eligible veterans and service members with full entitlement. Already used your benefit? Read our guide on [using your VA loan again](/posts/va-loan-entitlement-emerald-coast/).
- **Conventional loans:** As little as 3% down for some first-time buyers, with mortgage insurance that can be removed later.
- **FHA loans:** Flexible credit guidelines and 3.5% down for qualified buyers.
- **Jumbo loans:** For waterfront and higher-priced homes above $832,750.
