- **A HELOC is a revolving line of credit** secured by your home, usually with a variable rate.
- **Equity is near record levels:** $204,000 average tappable equity per borrower in Q3 2026 (ICE).
- **A HELOC keeps your first mortgage in place**, which matters when today's rates are above many existing loans.

## What Is a HELOC and How Does It Work?

According to the [Consumer Financial Protection Bureau](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-is-a-home-equity-line-of-credit-heloc-en-107/), a HELOC works in two phases:

- **Draw period.** You can borrow, repay and borrow again up to your credit limit, often for years. Some plans allow interest-only payments during this time.
- **Repayment period.** You can no longer draw, and you repay the balance, often with higher principal-and-interest payments.

**Tappable equity** is the amount you could borrow while still leaving a cushion of equity in the home. Lenders set their own limits on how much of your value can be borrowed in total.

## How Much Equity Do Homeowners Have Right Now?

The [ICE November 2026 Mortgage Monitor](https://mortgagetech.ice.com/resources/data-reports/november-2026-mortgage-monitor), released November 9, 2026, found:

- Mortgage holders held **$17.5 trillion** in equity in the third quarter, up 1.4% year over year.
- Average tappable equity was **$204,000** per borrower, keeping a 20% cushion.
- **Florida had 104,000** "equity millionaires," fourth among states.
- Cash-out refinances made up about **1 in 7** recent rate locks.

These are national figures. Your equity depends on your home's current value and what you owe. Our [Emerald Coast housing market update](/posts/emerald-coast-housing-market-november-2026/) shows how local prices have moved.

## Why Use a HELOC Instead of a Cash-Out Refinance?

Many homeowners locked in their first mortgage when rates were lower. Freddie Mac's 30-year fixed average was **7.11%** on November 12, 2026, and Mortgage News Daily's daily index was **7.51%** for a top-tier scenario on November 16, 2026. A cash-out refinance replaces your whole loan at today's rate. A HELOC only adds a second line on top.

That said, HELOC rates are usually variable, so payments can rise. Jay will compare the total cost of each option, including closing costs, before you decide.

## Which Storm-Ready Upgrades Are Worth Considering?

Common projects Emerald Coast owners plan in the off-season include:

- **Impact windows and doors** or rated storm shutters.
- **A new roof** or roof-to-wall connection upgrades.
- **Garage door bracing** and other opening protection.
- **Drainage and elevation work** in low-lying areas.

Angee's note from the buyer side: when homes go on the market, buyers and their insurance agents often ask about roof age and opening protection. Keep permits, invoices and any wind mitigation inspection reports. They help when you insure the home now and when you sell later.

### Check My Safe Florida Home first

Florida's [My Safe Florida Home](https://support.mysafeflhome.com/en/support/solutions/articles/156000390446-msfh-program-modernization-faqs) program said its current applicant portal would be temporarily unavailable beginning November 10, with an enhanced portal anticipated to launch December 4, 2026. If you are planning hardening work, check the program's status and rules before you start, since a HELOC could cover costs that a grant does not.

## Is HELOC Interest Tax Deductible?

Sometimes. [IRS Publication 936](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p936) says interest on home equity loans and lines of credit is deductible only if the borrowed funds are used to buy, build or substantially improve the home that secures the loan, and you must itemize. Using a HELOC for a car or a vacation does not qualify. This is general information, not tax advice.

## When Does a HELOC Make Sense?

A HELOC may fit if you:

- Have a low first-mortgage rate you want to keep.
- Have a project with costs spread over months.
- Have steady income and can handle a variable payment.

It may not fit if you would struggle with a payment increase or plan to sell soon. Remember that your home secures the line.
