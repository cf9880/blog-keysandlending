## What Makes Destin Special?

Destin calls itself **"the World's Luckiest Fishing Village,"** and the name comes from real history.

- **A New England fisherman founded it.** Captain Leonard Destin of New London, Connecticut, settled at Moreno Point and began fishing from East Pass. Local historian H.C. "Hank" Klein documented 1852 as the year he put down roots, according to The Destin Log.
- **The name came from a post office.** In 1896, an application for a post office at East Pass was rejected because the government wanted a one-word name. It was resubmitted as "Destin," according to the Northwest Florida Daily News history timeline.
- **The fishing rodeo is a long tradition.** The first Destin Fishing Rodeo launched in 1948 to bring in visitors.
- **Condos arrived in the 1970s.** Construction on Sandpiper Cove, Destin's first condominiums, began in 1973.
- **The city is younger than you'd think.** Destin incorporated as a city in 1984, according to the [City of Destin](https://www.cityofdestin.com/119/History-of-Destin).

The city also says it has the largest, most fully equipped fishing fleet in Florida, and that the 100-fathom curve comes closer to Destin than anywhere else in the state, giving charter boats quick access to deep water.

### Fun ways to get to know Destin

- **Walk Destin Harbor** and watch the charter fleet come in, a good way to see the town's working waterfront.
- **Spend an afternoon at Henderson Beach State Park** for dunes and white sand inside the city.

## What Should Condo Buyers Know in Destin?

Florida tightened condo safety rules after 2021. Here's what buyers will see:

- **Milestone inspections:** buildings three habitable stories or taller must have a milestone inspection by December 31 of the year the building reaches 30 years old, then every 10 years, under [Section 553.899, Florida Statutes](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/553.899).
- **Structural integrity reserve studies (SIRS):** associations must complete a SIRS at least every 10 years. The state's condo division says associations with a milestone inspection due by December 31, 2026 may complete both together, but no later than that date.
- **Seller disclosures:** under Section 718.503, sellers must give buyers the latest milestone inspection summary, if one applies, and the latest SIRS, or a statement that one isn't required or wasn't completed.

Jay's take: read the budget and reserve documents before you write an offer. A building that's behind on reserves may plan special assessments, and lenders review condo projects before they approve a loan.

## How Do You Finance a Home in Destin?

Loan choice depends on how you'll use the home:

- **Primary residence:** conventional, FHA, VA, and jumbo options. Okaloosa County's 2027 conforming limit is **$855,400** for one unit, up from $832,750, per FHFA.
- **Second home:** often financed with a conventional loan if it meets second-home rules. It can't be used as a full-time rental.
- **Military buyers:** VA loans can help with no down payment for eligible borrowers with full entitlement. See our [Eglin and Hurlburt PCS guide](/posts/buying-home-near-eglin-afb-hurlburt-field/).
- **Higher-priced homes:** loans above $855,400 are usually jumbo loans, which often need more down and more reserves.

Rates eased into Thanksgiving. Freddie Mac's 30-year fixed averaged **7.21%** as of November 25, 2026, down from 7.28% a week earlier. These are national averages, not offers.

### Budget for insurance

Coastal homes and condos can carry higher insurance costs. Ask for quotes on wind, flood, and the condo association's master policy before you commit, because they affect your payment and your approval.
