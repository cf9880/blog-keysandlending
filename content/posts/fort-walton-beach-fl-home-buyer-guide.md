## What Is Fort Walton Beach Known For?

Fort Walton Beach sits on the mainland side of the Santa Rosa Sound, across the Brooks Bridge from Okaloosa Island. The town was incorporated as Fort Walton in 1941 and renamed Fort Walton Beach in 1953.

Its best-known landmark is the [Heritage Park & Cultural Center](https://fwb.org/521/Heritage-Park-and-Cultural-Center-museum), run by the city. The park includes the Indian Temple Mound Museum, the Fort Walton Mound, the Camp Walton Schoolhouse, the Garnier Post Office, and a Civil War exhibit building. The city says the mound was built roughly between 800 and 1400 AD, and it was named a National Historic Landmark in 1964.

The city also has deep military ties. Hurlburt Field sits just west of town, and Eglin AFB's main base is a short drive to the northeast, so many residents have manageable commutes to base.

## What Loans Work Best in Fort Walton Beach?

**VA loans:** Eligible veterans and service members can often buy with no down payment and no monthly mortgage insurance. There's no VA loan limit for borrowers with full entitlement, though a one-time funding fee usually applies unless you're exempt.

**FHA loans:** Buyers with a 580+ credit score can put 3.5% down, up to Okaloosa County's 2026 limit of $603,750.

**Conventional loans:** Qualified buyers may put as little as 3% to 5% down, up to the $832,750 conforming limit.

Down payment assistance may also be available through several of Jay's lender partners, subject to eligibility.

## What Should Fort Walton Beach Buyers Know?

1. **Check flood zones.** Homes near the sound, the bayous, and on Okaloosa Island may need flood insurance. Get a quote before you make an offer.
2. **Ask about wind mitigation.** A current wind mitigation report can lower your insurance cost and your monthly payment.
3. **Island vs. mainland.** Some Okaloosa Island properties sit on leased land, similar to Pensacola Beach. Our [Pensacola Beach leasehold guide](/posts/pensacola-beach-leasehold-explained/) explains how leasehold works with financing.
4. **PCS timing.** If you're relocating to Hurlburt or Eglin, our [Eglin AFB and Hurlburt Field PCS guide](/posts/buying-home-near-eglin-afb-hurlburt-field/) covers timelines and VA tips.
5. **Consider the holidays.** Fewer buyers shop between Thanksgiving and New Year's, which can mean more room to negotiate.
