**First-time home buyers on the Emerald Coast can often start with 3% to 3.5% down, or no down payment with a VA or USDA loan if eligible. October 2026 single-family medians were $299,900 in Escambia, $345,000 in Santa Rosa, and $374,000 in Okaloosa County, and nearly half of active listings had cut their price.**

## Key Takeaways

- **Prices vary by county:** October 2026 single-family medians ranged from $299,900 (Escambia) to $374,000 (Okaloosa).
- **Low down payments exist:** 3% conventional, 3.5% FHA, and 0% VA or USDA for eligible buyers.
- **Buyers have leverage:** About 48% of active listings had cut their price as of Nov. 23, 2026.
- **Florida homestead exemption:** For 2026, $25,000 off all property taxes plus up to $26,411 off non-school taxes. Apply by March 1.
- **Rates are above 7%:** Freddie Mac's 30-year average was 7.28% on Nov. 19, 2026.

## Which Loans Work Best for First-Time Buyers?

Jay compares options from multiple lenders, so you can see the trade-offs side by side.

| Loan type | Minimum down payment | Good fit for |
|---|---|---|
| Conventional | 3% on some programs (Fannie Mae allows up to 97% LTV for eligible primary homes) | Solid credit, wanting to drop mortgage insurance later |
| FHA | 3.5% with a 580+ credit score | Lower scores or higher debt-to-income |
| VA | Often 0% for eligible veterans and service members | Military families, including Eglin and Hurlburt |
| USDA | 0% in eligible areas, with income limits | Homes in eligible rural parts of the region |

**LTV (loan-to-value)** is your loan amount divided by the home's value. A 97% LTV means a 3% down payment. **Mortgage insurance** protects the lender when you put less than 20% down on a conventional loan, and FHA loans carry their own mortgage insurance premium.

## How Does Florida's Homestead Exemption Help First-Time Buyers?

Florida's **homestead exemption** can lower property taxes on your primary home. According to the [Florida Department of Revenue](https://floridarevenue.com/property/Documents/PT113.pdf) and [section 196.031, Florida Statutes](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/196.031):

- The first $25,000 of assessed value is exempt from all property taxes, including school taxes.
- An additional exemption applies to assessed value above $50,000, for non-school taxes only. It is now adjusted for inflation each year and is **$26,411 for 2026**, per the Department of Revenue.
- You generally must own and live in the home as your permanent residence on **January 1** and apply with your county property appraiser by **March 1**.

That's one reason some buyers like closing before year-end. Ask your county property appraiser about deadlines that apply to you.

## What Is the Step-by-Step Plan for Buying Your First Home?

1. **Check your credit and budget.** Know your monthly comfort zone, including taxes, insurance, and HOA dues.
2. **Get preapproved.** A preapproval shows sellers you're ready and sets your price range.
3. **Pick your area.** Commute, schools, flood zones, and insurance costs all matter on the coast.
4. **Tour with a local REALTOR®.** A local agent can help you spot insurance, flood zone, or inspection questions early.
5. **Negotiate.** With many listings showing price cuts, ask about seller help with closing costs or a rate buydown.
6. **Close as a team.** When your agent and lender work together, the appraisal, inspection, and closing date stay in sync. See how our [realtor-lender team approach works](/posts/realtor-lender-team-pensacola-closing/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much do I need to put down as a first-time buyer on the Emerald Coast?

It depends on the loan. Some conventional programs allow 3% down, FHA allows 3.5% down with a 580 or higher credit score, and VA and USDA loans can offer no down payment for eligible buyers and properties.

### What do homes cost on the Emerald Coast right now?

In October 2026, single-family medians were $299,900 in Escambia County, $345,000 in Santa Rosa County and $374,000 in Okaloosa County, according to Momentum Realty's Market Pulse reports.

### Is down payment assistance available on the Emerald Coast?

Coastal Funding offers down payment assistance options through several of its lender partners. Eligibility depends on the program, your income, credit and the property, so ask Jay to review what may fit.

### What is the Florida homestead exemption?

For 2026, a Florida homestead gets a $25,000 exemption from all property taxes plus an additional exemption of up to $26,411 from non-school taxes on assessed value above $50,000. You generally must own and live in the home on January 1 and apply by March 1.

### Should first-time buyers wait until spring to buy?

Not necessarily. Fewer buyers shop during the holidays, and about 48% of active listings in both the Pensacola and Emerald Coast MLS areas had cut their price as of November 23, 2026.
